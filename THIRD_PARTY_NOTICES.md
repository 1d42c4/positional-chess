# Third-party artwork

The PDFs use the Cburnett chess piece artwork by Colin M. L. Burnett, supplied through python-chess 1.11.2. The piece artwork is licensed under the GNU General Public License, version 2 or later.

The corresponding SVG artwork is included in [source/cburnett](source/cburnett/), together with [COPYING.txt](source/cburnett/COPYING.txt). The upstream artwork definitions are in [python-chess chess/svg.py](https://github.com/niklasf/python-chess/blob/master/chess/svg.py). Color substitutions used in the PDFs: dark `#111827`, light `#f8f7ec`; board squares `#789656` and `#eff0d3`.

The original lesson text and constructed teaching positions are AI-created learning material prepared for 1d42c4. The source text is included in [source/lessons.json](source/lessons.json). The artwork license notice applies to the third-party artwork; no additional blanket license for the original material is granted here.
